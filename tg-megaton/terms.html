Megaton Terms

Last updated: August 1, 2026.

Digital items

Megaton sells optional digital items inside the Telegram Mini App using Telegram Stars and, where shown, TON or non-withdrawable in-game TON credit. Items add in-game caps, warhead upgrades, or collectible payloads. A purchase is delivered only after the server verifies the paid invoice, matching TON transfer, or atomic in-game credit debit.

Paid collectible odds

Paid collectible outcomes are selected on the server and stored in an immutable purchase receipt. Reopening the same purchase returns the original result; it does not reroll it.

  • Premium Payload, Premium x10, and the Starter Cache payload: 78% Rare, 16% Epic, 5.2% Legendary, 0.8% Mythic per roll.
  • Legendary Payload: 90% Legendary, 10% Mythic.

Premium x10 contains ten independently selected Premium rolls. Duplicate collectibles increase that item's copy count and may award the duplicate currency shown in the game. Collectibles and game currency have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn.

Refunds and support

Digital items are normally final once delivered, except where applicable law or Telegram policy requires otherwise. For purchase help, open @gamesfactorybot and send /paysupport with the payment problem and approximate purchase time.

Ads

Rewarded ads are optional. Rewards are granted only after the ad provider reports completion. If rewarded ads are unavailable, Megaton does not grant the extra rewarded-ad bonus.

Local save

General progress is mirrored between the Telegram WebView and the game's cloud save. Paid collectible receipts and paid-copy inventory are stored server-side and are restored from an authenticated inventory snapshot. Clearing local app data can reset unsynced local progress, but it does not reroll or erase a verified paid receipt.

Contact

Payment support: @gamesfactorybot via /paysupport. General support: @gamesfactorybot via /support.
